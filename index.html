System Overview

3-Layer Email Architecture — US Wellness Brand

Built for a US market launch: a full email lifecycle system segmented by funnel stage. Each layer has its own tone temperature, hook type, and objective — so no email sounds like it belongs to a different brand than the next.

1

Layer 1 — Acquisition / Onboarding (EN)

Cold to warm. Opens with a problem reframe — not a product pitch. Tone: direct, educational, curious. Designed to earn the right to the next email before asking for anything.

Subject: "You're not an insomniac. Here's what's actually happening."
Hook: PROBLEM · Goal: open + click · Target: 25%+ open rate
Layer 1 · EN
2

Layer 2 — Educación / Consideración (ES)

Ciencia + historia. Explica el mecanismo del producto sin sonar a manual técnico. Tono más lento, más profundo. El objetivo es construir confianza y desarmar objeciones antes del CTA de compra.

Asunto: "La melatonina no arregló tu sueño. Y hay una razón para eso."
Hook: REENCUADRE + CURIOSIDAD · Meta: consideración · Target: 4%+ CTR

Layer 3 — Retention / Loyalty (EN)

Post-purchase ritual. The email becomes a relationship — reinforcing habit, deepening brand identity, creating community around the product experience. Founder voice prominent. Sells the next purchase through belonging, not urgency.

How I build email systems

Voice architecture first

Before writing a single email, I map the brand lexicon — what words belong, what's prohibited, and how tone shifts across funnel stages. Consistency at scale requires a system, not just good instincts.

A/B-ready from the start

Subject lines and hooks are written in testable variations from day one. I document what was tested, what won, and why — so the next campaign starts from a higher baseline.

Bilingual by design

ES and EN are not translations of each other — they're distinct voices for distinct audiences. I write both from scratch, matching rhythm, tone, and cultural register to each language.

Paid Ad Copy

Performance · Direct Response
Framework

My ad copy is structured around a 4-step attention framework: pattern interrupt → emotional curiosity → emotional alignment → persuasion. The goal is to make people feel before they think. Most brands start at step 4. That's why their ads don't convert. Copy that earns the reader's attention before it asks for their money performs consistently better across funnel stages.

How I approach performance copy

Funnel-matched messaging

Cold audiences need pattern interrupts and emotional alignment. Warm audiences need proof and objection handling. Conversion audiences need frictionless offers. I never run bottom-funnel copy at cold traffic.

Compliance-aware

Health and wellness brands require careful language. I work within FTC-safe and INVIMA-safe frameworks — "supports / promotes / designed for" — and build compliant copy that still converts.

Platform-native always

Meta copy and TikTok copy are different formats with different rules. I write for each platform natively — not repurposed — because the algorithm rewards content that fits the context.
